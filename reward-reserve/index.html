How it works

The Reward Reserve holds TEN the way the Permanent Reserve does: it goes in and never comes out. Because it stays a holder, PAR keeps paying it rewards. The NET it receives is staked into sNET and becomes permanent principal. What that principal earns is the only thing that can ever be shared with holders.

  1. Hold TENTEN locked in the reserve, forever
  2. PAR organic rewardsTEN + NET paid to the reserve as a holder
  3. NET processedprocessNet() stakes NET on NetNet
  4. sNET principal100% of the sNET received is principal
  5. Productive growththe principal grows as sNET rebases
  6. 50/50 crystallizationat the start of a Reward cycle
  7. Reward potthe 50% that leaves principal
  8. Holder allocationsup to 10% of the pot per 24h epoch
PAR organic rewards

What the reserve's locked TEN earns as a PAR holder, in TEN and NET. Used to measure the rolling 7-day organic rate. When the NET is processed, all of the resulting sNET becomes principal. PAR rewards are never Reward Pot.

sNET productive yield

The growth of the staked principal after its last checkpoint. This is the only value eligible for the 50/50 split. Half strengthens principal for good, half becomes Reward Pot.

01
Compound Mode · rate at or above 10%PAR is paying well. Nothing is allocated. The principal keeps compounding and its growth waits, uncrystallized.
02
Entering Reward Mode · rate below 10%The growth since the last checkpoint is crystallized: 50% stays in principal, 50% moves to the Reward Pot.
03
Reward Mode · one epoch per 24hEach epoch commits up to 10% of the live pot to eligible holders, published as a Merkle root on chain.
04
Back to CompoundWhen organic rewards recover, new allocations stop. The rest of the pot waits for the next Reward Mode. What was already allocated stays claimable, even if you sold.

Why two modes: when PAR pays little, the reserve pays holders from what its principal earned. When PAR pays well, the reserve keeps building the base that produces the next rewards. A 24-hour minimum dwell keeps it from flapping.

Every purchase lot ages on its own: each full day after day 30 adds 0.01× until 2.00× at day 130. Selling or sending TEN removes the oldest lots first (FIFO). The Reward Reserve, the Permanent Reserve, LP and system addresses, and the production exclusion set never receive allocations.

Claim center · instructions only

This site never claims for you. It prepares the exact values; you send the transaction yourself on Blockscout, on the verified Reward Reserve contract, with the wallet that owns the reward. You pay only Robinhood Chain gas.

CHECK AN ADDRESSUse My TEN above to load the claim data for a public address.
How to claim
  1. Check your address above and confirm that CLAIMABLE NOW is greater than zero and the epoch shows VERIFIED.
  2. Click CLAIM ON BLOCKSCOUT.
  3. On Blockscout, open the verified Reward Reserve contract's Write tab (Contract → Write).
  4. Connect the wallet that owns this reward entitlement. The claim pays msg.sender: it only works from the address you checked.
  5. Open the method claim (selector 0xae0b51df).
  6. Enter the exact values shown by TenTen, in this order:
    • epochId (uint256): the epoch number
    • amount (uint256): the raw integer, exactly as shown. Do not press the ×1018 button.
    • proof (bytes32[]): one hash per field. Paste the first hash, press + to add a field for each next hash, keep the order.
  7. Optional: press Simulate first. Then press Write and confirm in your wallet.
  8. You pay only the network gas required by the transaction.
  9. After confirmation, come back here and check your address again.

One transaction claims the whole allocation of one epoch. Partial claims do not exist, and each epoch is claimed separately. amount is the face value fixed at publish time: the sNET actually paid is that amount scaled by sNET's growth since publish, so it can be slightly higher.
