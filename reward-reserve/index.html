How it works

TEN is held in your own wallet the whole time — this site never takes custody of it. Hold TEN for at least 30 days and you become eligible for a proportional share of the weekly sNET reward pot, published on chain as a Merkle root.

  1. Hold TEN30+ days in your own wallet
  2. Indexer snapshotseligibility & allocation computed off-chain
  3. Epoch publishedMerkle root committed on chain
  4. You checkthis site reads the epoch data for your address
  5. You claimone signed transaction, straight to your wallet
×This site never asks for a seed phrase or private key.
×No approval / allowance is ever required to claim: the contract sends sNET to you, it never pulls from you.
×Claiming never redirects to another domain.
✓Every allocation is checked against the contract's own verifyAllocation() before the claim button is enabled.
✓You sign every transaction yourself, in your own wallet.

Live on Robinhood Chain mainnet. Balance and eligibility reads work live against the contract; claiming works once an epoch is published — check the table below for your address.

Contracts · read them yourself

What this site reads or calls on the Reward Reserve contract:

claim(epochId, amount, proof)                          write · pays msg.sender, once per epoch
verifyAllocation(epochId, holder, amount, proof)       read · true if the proof matches the root
claimedBy(epochId, holder)                             read · true once claimed
epochs(epochId)              read · root, committedTotal, claimed, publishedAt, dataRef
principalValue()  holderSideValue()  outstandingLiabilityValue()  isSolvent()  latestEpochId()

No function moves TEN out of your wallet. This site never calls approve(). Verification status is read live from the explorer and shown only when it confirms it.
